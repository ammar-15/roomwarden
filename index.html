Shuttle requests by text

Housekeeping in one place

How the work moves

Every request follows the same simple path, and everyone who needs to know sees it as it happens.

  1. Guest texts for a ride

    They send where and when, like Terminal 1 at 6:15. No app, no phone call.

    Text received

  2. Desk and driver both see it

    The request lands on the front desk board and the driver's phone at the same time.

    New request

  3. Driver confirms

    One tap, and the guest gets a text back with their pickup time.

    Confirmed

  4. Ride gets logged

    Every trip is saved with the time and room, so you know your busy hours.

    Completed

  1. Guest checks out

    The room flips to dirty and goes on the right housekeeper's list.

    Dirty

  2. Housekeeper starts the room

    They tap it on their phone, so everyone can see it's being cleaned.

    Cleaning

  3. Marked clean

    Done in one tap. No radio call to the desk.

    Clean

  4. Inspected and ready

    The supervisor signs off and the front desk can assign it right away.

    Ready

    Front desk, housekeeping or a manager adds the room and what's wrong.

Questions we get a lot

What is Room Warden?

Room Warden is hotel operations software. It puts housekeeping, live room status, shuttle requests, maintenance tickets, guest requests, feedback and events in one web app your whole team can use.

Do I need to integrate it with my PMS?

No integration needed. Room Warden works on its own, so there's no PMS setup, no vendor approval and nothing to migrate. If you switch PMS later, your ops stay the same.

How do shuttle requests by text work?

Guests text your hotel's Room Warden number with where and when they need a ride. The request shows up for the front desk and the driver right away, and replies go back to the guest as a normal text.

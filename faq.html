Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about how Room Warden works, what it covers and how setup goes. Don't see your question? Ask us directly.

The basics

What is Room Warden?

Room Warden is hotel operations software. It puts housekeeping, live room status, shuttle requests, maintenance tickets, guest requests, feedback and events in one web app your whole team can use.

Is Room Warden a PMS?

No. It doesn't handle reservations, rates or billing. It covers the day-to-day operational work that most property management systems leave out, and it runs next to the PMS you already have.

Do I need to integrate it with my PMS?

No integration needed. Room Warden works on its own, so there's no PMS setup, no vendor approval and nothing to migrate. If you switch PMS later, your ops stay the same.

What kind of hotels is it for?

It's built with select-service and airport hotels in mind, especially properties that run a shuttle and still coordinate housekeeping over radio or phone. Any property that wants its operations in one place can use it.

Shuttle and housekeeping

How do shuttle requests by text work?

Guests text your hotel's Room Warden number with where and when they need a ride. The request shows up for the front desk and the driver right away, and replies go back to the guest as a normal text.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. Guests use regular text messages. Your staff use Room Warden in a web browser on any phone, tablet or computer, so nobody installs anything.

How do housekeepers update room status?

From their phone. They tap a room to mark it in progress, clean or inspected, and the front desk sees the change right away. No more radioing to ask if a room is ready.

Can we track maintenance history?

Yes. Every ticket keeps a record of who logged it, who worked on it and when it was closed, so you can spot rooms with repeat problems.

Setup, access and pricing

Can I control what each staff member sees?

Yes. Every person gets their own login, set up by an admin, and permissions are set per user. A driver sees shuttle requests, a housekeeper sees their rooms, and managers see everything.

How does setup work?

We set it up with you. We load your rooms, add your team and match the workflows to how your property already runs, then walk your staff through it.

Where is our data hosted?

On dedicated servers, with a second server for failover and regular backups, so the app stays up and your records stay safe.

How much does Room Warden cost?

Pricing depends on your property size and which workflows you use. Book a demo and we'll send you a quote that fits your hotel.
